# Finance Teams

## See every dollar — and plan with confidence.

#### Raindrop agentic AI unifies spend across suppliers, regions, and functions in one AI-native platform — so Finance can consolidate data, catch overruns before they happen, and turn spend into a forecast you can trust.

## The Problem

### Fragmented Spend Data

#### Disparate systems make it challenging to consolidate spend information, making accurate financial planning seem impossible.

### Uncontrolled Fiscal Commitments

#### Without real-time insights and easy access to information, organizations risk overspending due to unnoticed budget overruns or contract overconsumption.

### Limited Spend Transparency

#### Difficulty in accessing detailed spend analytics across suppliers, commodities, and regions impedes strategic decision-making.​

## The Solution

### Who It's For

#### Finance teams — FP&A, Controllers, and CFOs — that need one trustworthy view of spend to plan, control budgets, and find savings.

### What Finance sees

#### Contracted and non-contracted spend across suppliers, commodities, and regions — consolidated in real time in a single model.

### How Finance Stays In Control

#### Intake and approval workflows tie every commitment to budget before it’s made, and real-time alerts flag overruns before they land on the P&L.

### HOW RAINDROP HELPS

#### Unified Spend Visibility with Spend Intelligence: Consolidate spend data across suppliers, regions, and functions for comprehensive financial analysis and planning.

#### Real-Time Visibility and Alerts: Receive proactive notifications when budgets are nearing limits or when commodity consumption approaches contracted amounts.

#### Advanced, AI-driven analytics. Predict spending trends and identify cost-saving opportunities.

#### Seamless Integration: Quickly connect with existing ERP and financial systems, ensuring a smooth transition and minimal disruption. We’re seriously fast, just ask.

#### User-Friendly in every way: Intuitive design promotes widespread adoption across teams, reducing training time and increasing efficiency.​

### WHAT YOU GET WITH RAINDROP

#### Unified Intake Hub: One Digital Front Door for all commitment requests—purchases, sourcing events, and contract—your questions answered or routed automatically

#### Guided Sourcing, Built for Speed: Launch RFX events in minutes with out of the box templates, cloning events, or even just ask Rain to create one for you, and easily evaluate with automated scoring, and side-by-side bid evaluation.

#### Supplier Collaboration Made Easy: Work with vendors directly in-platform—no more email ping-pong or lost documents.

#### Sourcing-to-Contract Connection: Convert sourcing events into purchase orders or contracts in a click, with full traceability and zero data loss.

#### Compliance Without the Chase: Dynamic workflows keep teams aligned —without Procurement playing hall monitor.

#### Up and Running Fast: Go live in weeks, not months—with best-practice built in, Out of the box ERP and P2P integrations and no-code workflows built in.

#### Designed by Procurement, for Procurement: Every feature reflects firsthand knowledge of what works (and what doesn’t) in the real world.

### Out Comes That Matter

#### One consolidated source across suppliers, regions, and functions — no more forecasting as a data cleanup exercise. Real-time alerts fire as budgets or contract consumption approach their limits. AI-driven analytics surface spending trends and cost-saving opportunities across the portfolio.

### FAQ

#### Can Finance trust Raindrop’s AI-driven analytics and forecasts?Yes. The agentic AI works from your consolidated spend data and shows the basis for what it surfaces — trends and cost-saving opportunities your team can validate, not black-box numbers you have to take on faith. You keep control of the assumptions and the decisions.

#### How does Raindrop make spend data reliable enough to plan on?Raindrop unifies procurement, sourcing, contract, and invoice data into one model, so contracted and non-contracted spend reconcile in a single place — giving you one source of truth instead of stitched-together exports.

#### How does Raindrop support audit and financial controls?Every commitment runs through approval workflows with a full audit trail, and Raindrop is [SOC 2 compliant.](/security)

#### How does Raindrop help finance teams gain better visibility into spend?Raindrop provides real-time, comprehensive views of enterprise-wide spend — contracted and non-contracted — by unifying procurement, sourcing, and contract data. Finance can proactively monitor budgets, identify risks, and surface cost-saving opportunities.

#### Can Raindrop support budget control without adding manual work?Yes. With intelligent intake and approval workflows, Raindrop ensures every commitment — purchase, contract, or sourcing event — is pre-approved and aligned with budget policies. Automation and AI reduce manual reviews while increasing compliance.

#### How does Raindrop help prevent rogue or off-contract spend?Raindrop’s unified intake process is a digital front door for all spend requests, guiding employees to compliant suppliers and workflows. This centralizes purchasing activity, reduces shadow spend, and improves adherence to negotiated terms.

#### Is Raindrop compatible with our current financial systems and ERP?Yes. Raindrop integrates with a wide range of ERP and financial systems — invoice data, approvals, supplier onboarding — ensuring financial alignment and data continuity across platforms.

#### How quickly can finance teams see value after implementing Raindrop?Finance leaders typically begin seeing impact in weeks, not months. Because Raindrop is modular and easy to deploy, teams can prioritize high-value areas — invoice automation, budget control, or contract visibility — and expand as needed without heavy IT lift.
