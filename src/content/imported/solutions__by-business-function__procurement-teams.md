# Procurement Teams

## Bring all of procurement into one place — and put spend back under control.

#### Raindrop unifies intake, sourcing, suppliers, and contracts in one AI-native platform — so requests stop going around you, sourcing moves in minutes, and every dollar runs through a single digital front door.

## The Problem

### Fragmented Spend Data

#### Disparate systems make it challenging to consolidate spend information, making accurate financial planning seem impossible.

### Uncontrolled Fiscal Commitments

#### Without real-time insights and easy access to information, organizations risk overspending due to unnoticed budget overruns or contract overconsumption.

### Limited Spend Transparency

#### Difficulty in accessing detailed spend analytics across suppliers, commodities, and regions impedes strategic decision-making.​

## The Solution

### Who It's For

#### Procurement teams that need to centralize intake, speed up sourcing, and bring spend under control — without a heavy change-management project.

### What Procurement Runs

#### Intake, sourcing events, supplier relationships, contracts, and spend — all on one platform, built by procurement pros who’ve done the job.

### How Procurement stays in control

#### A single digital front door captures every request, routes it automatically, and enforces compliance through dynamic workflows — so nothing slips around the process and every commitment is traceable.

### HOW RAINDROP HELPS

#### Raindrop pairs [agentic AI](/agentic-procurement) with everything Procurement needs, in one intuitive platform—designed by Procurement pros who've been in your shoes. It's fast to deploy, easy to use, and powerful enough to centralize intake, automate sourcing, and drive contract compliance—with Rain, Raindrop's agentic AI agent, handling the busywork so your team can focus on decisions, all without heavy change management.

### WHAT YOU GET WITH RAINDROP

#### Unified Intake Hub: One Digital Front Door for all commitment requests—purchases, sourcing events, and contract—your questions answered or routed automatically

#### Guided Sourcing, Built for Speed: Launch RFX events in minutes with out of the box templates, cloning events, or even just ask Rain to create one for you, and easily evaluate with automated scoring, and side-by-side bid evaluation.

#### Supplier Collaboration Made Easy: Work with vendors directly in-platform—no more email ping-pong or lost documents.

#### Sourcing-to-Contract Connection: Convert sourcing events into purchase orders or contracts in a click, with full traceability and zero data loss.

#### Compliance Without the Chase: Dynamic workflows keep teams aligned —without Procurement playing hall monitor.

#### Up and Running Fast: Go live in weeks, not months—with best-practice built in, Out of the box ERP and P2P integrations and no-code workflows built in.

#### Designed by Procurement, for Procurement: Every feature reflects firsthand knowledge of what works (and what doesn’t) in the real world.

### How To Get Started

#### Raindrop offers a full suite of Source-to-Pay applications—including intake across the suite, sourcing, contracts, supplier management, spend intelligence, eProcurement and Accounts Payable. But you don’t have to start with everything. Thanks to our modular approach, you can begin where the need is greatest and expand as you go. Everything is built on a single code base on the Google Cloud Platform with a unified user experience, so as you add capabilities, your team won’t have to learn a new tool or adjust to a new interface.

#### It’s seamless, scalable, and built to grow with you.

### FAQ

#### How does Raindrop help streamline the entire procurement process?Raindrop brings all procurement activities—intake, sourcing, contract management, and approvals—into one intuitive platform. This eliminates fragmented tools and manual processes, enabling teams to move faster with greater control and visibility across every stage.

#### Can Raindrop help reduce rogue or off-contract spending?Yes. By providing a single digital front door for all purchasing and sourcing requests, Raindrop drives compliance from the start. Requests are automatically routed, tracked, and aligned with preferred suppliers and negotiated contracts, minimizing shadow spend.

#### What automation features are available to lighten the workload for procurement?Procurement teams can automate RFX events, contract workflows, and supplier onboarding using Raindrop’s templates and AI-powered tools. This reduces time spent on repetitive tasks and shortens sourcing cycles from weeks to minutes.

#### How does Raindrop improve collaboration across departments?Raindrop connects procurement with legal, finance, and stakeholders through shared workflows, notifications, and clear approval paths. Everyone stays on the same page, which speeds up decision-making and reduces back-and-forth confusion.

#### Will Raindrop integrate with our existing systems?Absolutely. Raindrop was built to integrate easily with ERPs, CLM systems, and finance tools. This ensures procurement data flows seamlessly across platforms, reducing manual data entry and enabling better spend and supplier analytics.
