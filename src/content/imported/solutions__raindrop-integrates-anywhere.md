## From Integration to Orchestration: The Distinction That Matters

#### Every procurement supplier claims integration. Most mean the same thing: data moves between systems on a schedule. Suppliers get synced overnight. Invoices get pushed in batches. Payment status updates in the morning.

#### That is not integration. That is data transfer with extra steps.

#### Real integration does three things the legacy model does not:

#### Unified data model. RainConnect creates a single view of your supplier, PO, invoice, and payment data, not three copies drifting out of sync. When a supplier’s banking details change in the ERP, they are corrected in the Raindrop platform instantly. When an invoice is approved in the platform, the ERP sees the match before the AP team does.

#### Process orchestration across systems. Intake in the platform triggers validation, routes through approvals, pushes to the ERP, and confirms activation, all without a human handoff. You do not integrate systems; you orchestrate work that happens to cross them.

#### Context-aware decisioning. AI adds judgment, not just routing. Rain interprets an unstructured invoice, flags a mismatch against the PO, and suggests a resolution. Your team reviews the edge cases. The rest moves on its own.

#### Integration moves data.Orchestration moves work.

#### Most legacy P2P suites give you the first. Raindrop gives you both.

## FAQ

#### How long does it take to integrate Raindrop with our ERP?A single module can go live in as little as 1 week; a full Source-to-Pay deployment typically takes 2 to 3 months (8 to 16 weeks). Module timing varies with scope, so treat 1 week as the fast case rather than a typical figure. Both numbers compare against the 8 to 12 months a legacy suite rollout usually takes.

#### Will connecting Raindrop disrupt our existing ERP?No. RainConnect integrates with the ERP you already run, whether that is NetSuite, SAP, Oracle, Dynamics 365, Workday, Sage, or another system. There is no rip-and-replace ERP project. Your ERP stays the system of record; Raindrop reads from it and writes back to it.

#### What ERPs does Raindrop actually connect to?NetSuite, Sage, Oracle (including Oracle Fusion and JD Edwards), SAP Ariba, SAP S/4HANA, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Microsoft Great Plains, and Workday all have maintained connections today. Raindrop's connector architecture is also built to reach ERPs beyond this list, so ask directly if yours is not named here.

#### How does Raindrop handle invoices with no purchase order?Machine learning auto-codes non-PO invoices, such as subscriptions, services, and one-off purchases, using historical coding patterns and your GL rules instead of routing them to a person's inbox to guess at.

#### Is RainConnect a native integration or a middleware connector?RainConnect is built into the core Raindrop platform from day one, not a separate product bolted on after go-live. Business users configure most connections through a low-code interface powered by Workato, and IT owns access, security, and governance rather than day-to-day integration plumbing.

#### Do we need IT or a consulting team to manage the integrations?Minimal IT involvement is required day to day. Most configuration happens through the low-code interface. IT typically supports initial access, security validation, and governance, but ongoing management does not depend on a consulting engagement or dedicated integration developers.

#### Is data synced in real time or on a batch schedule?Both, configurable per workflow. Approvals, invoice status, supplier updates, and deactivations can run in real time. Other data processes can run on a schedule when real-time sync is not needed. You choose the mode per workflow, not per platform.

#### How is Raindrop's integration different from traditional middleware?Traditional middleware stitches systems together after the fact and still leaves your team reconciling data manually. Raindrop provides a unified data model through RainConnect, orchestrates processes across systems rather than just passing data, and adds AI context and decisioning instead of routing alone.

#### Is our data secure across every integrated system?Yes. The Raindrop platform is built with enterprise-grade security and governance: role-based access controls, secure API communication, full audit trails across transactions and workflows, and compliance inherited from Google Cloud infrastructure.

#### Can integrations adapt as our tech stack changes?Yes. The platform's modular, API-first architecture lets integrations evolve as your stack changes. You can swap ERPs, add tools, or expand functionality without rebuilding the entire integration layer from scratch.
