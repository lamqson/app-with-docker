### Watch the savings rain down

#### Raindrop Sourcing is AI sourcing software that streamlines the entire sourcing process—from drafting RFPs to awarding contracts—leveraging AI to boost efficiency, drive savings, and reduce risk. Built for modern procurement teams, our strategic sourcing software helps you launch events faster, evaluate bids smarter, and unlock greater savings.

## KEY BENEFITS

### Rapid Go-to-Market

#### Launch sourcing events up to 75% faster with automated sourcing workflows, pre-built templates, and one-click cloning.

### AI-Driven Savings

#### Deliver real-time feedback to suppliers to encourage competitive bids and maximize cost reduction.

### Risk Mitigation

#### Assess supplier qualifications and compliance using AI-powered analysis—catch issues before they become problems.

## How It Works

## Collaborate Seamlessly

### FAQ

#### What types of sourcing events can I manage through Raindrop?Raindrop supports a wide range of sourcing events including RFX (RFPs, RFIs, RFQs), reverse auctions, blind bids, and direct negotiations. The platform is designed to be flexible, allowing procurement teams to create events of any complexity using templates, custom workflows, or AI-powered assistance.

#### How does raindrop streamline the sourcing process?Raindrop simplifies sourcing by automating repetitive tasks, centralizing communications, and enabling real-time collaboration. Users can launch events in minutes, compare bids side-by-side, and maintain complete visibility throughout the sourcing lifecycle—from intake to award.

#### Can I use templates or duplicate past events to save time?Yes. Raindrop offers configurable templates and cloning features so you can quickly replicate successful past events. This reduces administrative effort and ensures consistency across sourcing initiatives.

#### How does Raindrop support supplier engagement during events?Suppliers can access a dedicated portal to submit responses, ask clarifying questions, and receive real-time updates. Raindrop ensures transparency and fairness while simplifying supplier onboarding and communication.

#### Is it possible to track savings and performance from sourcing events?Absolutely. Raindrop provides robust analytics and reporting tools that track cost savings, supplier performance, event cycle times, and sourcing effectiveness—helping procurement teams measure impact and continuously improve.
