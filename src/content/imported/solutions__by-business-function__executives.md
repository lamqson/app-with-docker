# Executives

### WE KNOW YOUR PAIN

### Too Hard to Use

#### Most procurement and finance systems feel like they were designed against users—clunky, confusing, and full of friction.

### Always Waiting for Answers

#### Need to know the status of a request? A contract? A supplier? It often means emailing someone and hoping they respond.

### Too Much Admin, Too Little Time

#### Manual processes and approvals eat up hours that could be spent on real, strategic work.

### HOW RAINDROP HELPS

#### Raindrop gives business teams a consumer-grade experience in an enterprise platform. It’s intuitive, easy to adopt, and designed for a person to use, not an engineer. Stakeholders can request what they need, track progress, and access insights—all without emailing Procurement or Finance or Legal and waiting for a response. This all allows you to stop focusing on back office functions so you can do what you do best - your real job.

### WHAT YOU GET WITH RAINDROP

#### Consumer-Grade Interface: No training manuals needed. Raindrop feels familiar from the first click, in every way you work with it.

#### Smart Intake Portals: A guided Digital Front Door for purchases, sourcing events, contract requests, and more—configurable by team or role.

#### Self-Service Tracking: Instantly see the status of your requests, contracts, and suppliers—no more pinging people for updates.

#### Dynamic No-Code Workflows: Approvals, routing, notifications—handled behind the scenes, without slowing anyone down.

#### Real-Time Visibility: Analytics, dashboards, and even Rain, our AI Agent, put the information you need at your fingertips, no data team required.

### BONUS

#### Raindrop empowers your organization by providing a modular, unified platform where stakeholders only see what’s relevant to them—and they never have to learn a new tool when you add a new module.

### FAQ

#### How does Raindrop help executive teams make more informed decisions?Raindrop delivers real-time visibility into spend, supplier relationships, and sourcing activities. Executives can rely on accurate, centralized data to make faster, data-driven decisions that impact financial performance and strategic planning.

#### What measurable ROI can leadership expect from implementing Raindrop?Organizations using Raindrop typically see 10–20% cost savings across indirect spend categories. Additionally, the platform reduces cycle times, improves compliance, and increases efficiency—freeing up resources for higher-value strategic initiatives.

#### Can Raindrop support enterprise-wide transformation initiatives?Yes. Raindrop is designed to integrate across business units, breaking down silos between procurement, finance, and operations. This enables cohesive execution of strategic initiatives such as digital transformation, cost optimization, and ESG compliance.

#### How does Raindrop mitigate business risk at the executive level?Raindrop automates policy enforcement, ensures supplier compliance, and provides full audit trails—minimizing risk exposure and improving accountability. Executives gain confidence in both governance and operational resilience.

#### What makes Raindrop different from traditional procurement tools?Unlike legacy systems that require extensive change management, Raindrop is modern, intuitive, and quick to deploy. It’s designed for adoption across the organization, making it easier for leadership to drive value creation without disrupting current workflows.
