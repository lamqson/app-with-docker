# Contract Lifecycle Management

### Steer The Storm: Turn Commitments Into Clarity

#### Contracts shouldn't live in silos. Yet for most Procurement and Legal teams, they do — scattered across email threads, shared drives, and a legacy CLM system nobody quite trusts. Every contract you sign is a commitment your business has to honor.

#### Raindrop Systems Contract Lifecycle Management brings authoring, review, approval, signature, and obligation tracking into one AI-native contract lifecycle management platform — connected to the rest of your Source-to-Pay workflow. So contracts stop being static files and start working as live commitments tied to spend, suppliers, and outcomes. That's contract lifecycle management software done the way procurement and finance leaders actually need it.

## The contract problem most teams know too well

### Contracts are scattered everywhere

#### Active agreements live across inboxes, shared drives, a legacy CLM, your eSignature tool, and someone’s desktop. There’s no single source of truth — and the clause that matters is always one Slack thread away from being lost forever.

### Status, ownership, and obligations are invisible

#### Who owns this renewal? When does it expire? What did we commit to in the SOW? Most teams answer these by asking around — not querying their system. Obligations get missed because nobody knows they exist.

### Negotiation cycles drag on

#### Manual redlining, version-control chaos, and email-based reviews stretch a 5-day negotiation into a 5-week one. Legal becomes the bottleneck. Procurement waits. Deals slip.

### Contracts disconnect from procurement and finance

#### The signed agreement says one price. The PO says another. The invoice says a third. Without contracts wired into sourcing, eProcurement, and AP systems through real ERP contract management integration, you’re catching leakage in audits, not preventing it in workflow.

### Compliance and scope are unenforceable

#### You negotiated favorable pricing, payment terms, SLAs, and rebates. None of that is enforced unless someone manually checks every invoice and milestone. Most teams don’t have the capacity. So commitments quietly go unmet — and scope creep walks in unchallenged.

### Shadow and rogue contracts pile up off-system

#### When intake is hard, people work around it. Contracts get signed in inboxes, marketing tools, and sales platforms — outside the approved process and outside any central view. Legal finds out at renewal. Finance finds out at audit. Procurement finds out when the invoice doesn’t match anything on file.

## How It Works

## AI Powered Redlining In MS Word

#### With the Raindrop Word Add-In, Legal, Procurement, and business teams can use Rain, Raindrop’s AI agent, to analyze contracts, flag risk, and suggest language replacements without ever leaving Microsoft Word.

## Third Party Contract REview

#### Supplier paper does not have to slow the contract process down. Rain AI reviews third-party agreements, flags risk, suggests redlines, and helps Procurement and Legal teams move from review to resolution with greater speed, consistency, and control.

## One Click NDA Creation

#### Simply pull up the supplier, select the signatories, and submit. Raindrop automatically generates the NDA, routes it for signature through [RainSign](/solutions/modules/rainsign) (Raindrop’s built-in document signing solution), and links it back to the supplier record — keeping everything connected and visible in one place.

## WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

### From Documents to Commitments

#### Most CLM platforms are sophisticated filing cabinets. Raindrop Systems treats every contract as an active commitment. Renewal dates, payment terms, SLAs, pricing, and obligations are extracted, tracked, and tied to your spend data. The platform tells you when a milestone is approaching — before it costs you.

### AI Built In, Not Bolted On

#### Raindrop Systems was built AI-native from the start. Rain, our purpose-built procurement AI agent, drafts contracts, redlines against your playbook, surfaces risky clauses, and answers natural-language questions about your contract base. It’s not a feature you turn on. It’s how the platform works.

### Connected Source-to-Pay Platform

#### A standalone CLM forces you to integrate it back into procurement. Raindrop Systems Contract Management is one module in a unified S2P platform. Contracts connect natively to sourcing events, supplier records, requisitions, POs, and invoices. No rebuilds. No reconciliation projects. No swivel-chairing between vendors.

#### What is contract lifecycle management (CLM)?Contract lifecycle management is the process of managing every contract your organization signs — from initial request and drafting, through negotiation and approval, to execution, performance tracking, and renewal or expiration. CLM software brings those stages into one platform so contracts can be authored, reviewed, signed, stored, and acted on without scattering across tools. The goal isn't just storage; it's visibility into commitments, faster cycles, and enforcement of the terms you negotiated.

#### How is Raindrop different from traditional CLM tools?Traditional CLM platforms are document-centric: they store, search, and route contracts. Raindrop Systems is commitment-centric — every contract becomes a live record of obligations, milestones, and value tied directly to your sourcing and spend data. AI is built in from the ground up, not added as a bolted-on module. And because Raindrop Systems is one part of a unified Source-to-Pay platform, contracts connect natively to your suppliers, sourcing events, requisitions, and invoices. No standalone integration project. No swivel-chairing between vendors.

#### Can Raindrop integrate with eSignature tools?Yes. The Raindrop Systems platform integrates natively with DocuSign and Adobe Sign — the two most common enterprise eSignature platforms — and includes RainSign for fully embedded signature workflows. Signed contracts return to the repository automatically with version control intact, so you never have to upload a signed PDF or reconcile two versions of the same agreement.

#### How does AI improve contract management?AI in the Raindrop Systems platform works in three ways. First, Rain drafts contracts from your clause library and playbook — pre-populating standard terms so Legal can focus on review, not formatting. Second, AI redlines incoming contracts against your standards, flagging non-standard or risky language during review rather than at audit. Third, AI extracts and tracks obligations across your entire contract base, monitoring renewals, milestones, and compliance continuously. The outcome: faster cycles, fewer missed obligations, and contract data you can actually query.

#### Can we track contract obligations?Yes — and this is where most legacy CLM platforms fall short. Raindrop Systems extracts contract terms (renewal dates, payment terms, SLAs, volume commitments, rebates, exclusivity clauses) at the point of signature and tracks them through execution. When an obligation is approaching, slipping, or being violated by an invoice, the platform notifies the right owner. Obligations stop being something you discover during an audit and become something you manage in real time.

#### How does CLM connect to procurement and finance?Because the Raindrop Systems platform is unified, contracts are wired into sourcing events, supplier records, requisitions, POs, and AP automation. When a sourcing event awards a supplier, the contract is created from the event. When an invoice arrives, it's matched against contract terms automatically. When a renewal is due, the procurement team sees it alongside the supplier's spend history, performance, and risk. Finance sees commitments tied to forecasted spend. There's no integration project — it's one platform, one data model.

#### How long does implementation take?Raindrop Systems is built for fast, phased deployments. Most customers see the Contract Management module live in 2–4 weeks, with the full Source-to-Pay platform deployed in a few months, not years. We achieve this because the platform is AI-native and configuration-led — not a years-long custom build with six-figure consulting overhead.

#### Do legal teams need to manage every contract?No, and that's part of the point. Raindrop Systems lets Legal define the playbook — clause libraries, approval thresholds, risk rules, pre-approved templates — and then provides self-service contract generation for business users within those guardrails. Standard NDAs and SOWs route to signature automatically; anything that deviates from the playbook escalates to Legal for human review. Non-lawyers can safely draft compliant contracts because the guardrails are baked into the workflow, not enforced through email chains. The result: Legal stops being the bottleneck on every NDA, MSA, and SOW renewal, and gets to spend its time on the contracts that actually matter.

#### How does Raindrop compare to legacy CLM platforms?Legacy CLM platforms — point solutions and ERP-bolted contract modules — were designed for an era when contracts lived in legal departments and the rest of the business worked around them. Raindrop Systems was built for a different reality: contracts are commitments that span Legal, Procurement, Finance, and the business, and they need to connect to the systems where work actually happens. Where legacy tools require manual handoffs, custom integrations, and AI add-ons, Raindrop Systems delivers AI-native authoring, native S2P connectivity, and faster deployments out of the box.

#### Is contract data secure?Yes. The Raindrop Systems platform is hosted on Google Cloud Platform with enterprise-grade encryption in transit and at rest, and is SOC 2 Type II compliant. We support SSO, role-based access controls, structured audit logging for every approval and decision, and configurable data residency. Contract data — often the most sensitive information your organization holds — is protected to meet the security and compliance requirements of regulated industries, including financial services and healthcare. Your IT and security teams get the controls they need; your Legal and Procurement teams get the workflows they need.
