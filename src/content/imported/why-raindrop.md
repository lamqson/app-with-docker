# Why Raindrop?

### THE RAINDROP DIFFERENCE

#### We know modern procurement is about more than just saving money — it’s about upholding commitments.Built for speed, flexibility, and clarity, Raindrop is here to help you best meet your business commitments throughout the entire Procurement lifecycle, from initial intake through supplier management, sourcing and contracts to ordering, invoice automation and payments — all while being compliant.

#### We bring deep experience across procurement, finance, product development, and AI, so we understand your challenges firsthand. Every feature in Raindrop is purpose-built to eliminate those pain points and support the fast-moving needs of innovative companies.

### [on Our Expertise on Our Expertise](/why-raindrop/our-expertise)Raindrop Redefines Spend Management

### Raindrop

#### Lightning-Fast DeploymentModules live in as little as two weeks, full suite in three months with no consultants needed.

#### User-Centric Design and Tailored FeaturesModern, intuitive UI built by spend management experts. Explore hundreds of pre-built templates and limitless configurations to tailor the platform to how you work best.

#### Unified & Integrated PlatformRaindrop replaces silos with an AI-powered, full-suite, source-to-pay platform that streamlines the entire spend management lifecycle and integrates seamlessly with existing systems.

#### Commitment-Driven Value CreationRaindrop empowers strategic decision-making and real business value, beyond just cost savings.

### Legacy Vendors

#### Tedious ImplementationTraditional tools take months to roll out and often require third-party consultants.

#### Rigid InterfacesInflexible systems not built with the end user in mind and difficult to integrate.

#### Complex, Siloed SolutionsLegacy platforms offer fragmented point solutions that address only one problem at a time.

#### Focus on Cost-Cutting AloneLegacy tools emphasize savings at the expense of strategic value.

#### “Raindrop is an industry-disruptive digital spend management platform, designed to act as the central hub capable of managing all outflow of funds from a customer’s company. Founded by highly experienced procurement practitioners, technologists, and operational experts, Raindrop brings a fresh user experience to a mature industry in need of change”
