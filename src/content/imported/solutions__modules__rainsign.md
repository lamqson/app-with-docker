# RainSign

### Sign-off without the storm

#### RainSign by Raindrop streamlines the entire contract signature process—from initiation to execution—within a single, secure platform. Designed for seamless integration across sourcing, procurement, and legal workflows, RainSign accelerates approvals, reduces costs, and enhances compliance.

## KEY BENEFITS

### Accelerate Contract Execution

#### Eliminate delays with intuitive digital signatures, enabling faster turnaround times and improved efficiency.​

### Enhance Security and Compliance

#### Ensure every signature is legally binding and auditable, meeting global compliance standards.​

### Reduce Operational Costs

#### Cut expenses associated with printing, scanning, and mailing by transitioning to a fully digital signature process, at a fraction of the cost of other solutions

## How It Works

## Initiate Signing in Raindrop

#### Start a contract in Rain and send it for signature with a single click. Simple, fast, and ready to go.

## Audit Ready, Always

#### Every signature is tracked, time-stamped, and easy to trace.

## Monitor Progress

#### Track the status of each document in real-time, with notifications upon completion.

### Seamless Integration Across Workflows

#### RainSign integrates effortlessly with Raindrop’s suite of solutions, providing:​

#### Unified contract management from creation to signature.

#### Consistent user experience across procurement and legal teams.

#### Centralized storage and retrieval of all signed documents.

### FAQ

#### What is Rainsign and how does it help with electronic signatures?RainSign is Raindrop’s built-in e-signature solution that enables users to generate, send, and track legally binding digital signatures for contracts and documents. It eliminates the need for third-party signature tools and integrates seamlessly with contract workflows within the Raindrop platform.

#### How does Rainsign integrate with contract management in Raindrop?RainSign is natively embedded in Raindrop’s Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM) module, allowing users to initiate signature workflows directly from within a contract record. This ensures end-to-end visibility, version control, and automatic storage of signed documents.

#### Is Rainsign legally compliant and secure for contract signing?Yes, RainSign adheres to global e-signature laws such as the U.S. ESIGN Act and eIDAS in the EU. It offers audit trails, multi-factor authentication, and encryption protocols to ensure both security and compliance.

#### Can I track the status of a document sent through Rainsign?Absolutely. RainSign provides real-time tracking of signature status, including notifications for when a document is viewed, signed, or completed. This helps accelerate deal cycles and improves accountability.

#### Do I need a separate license or integration to use Rainsign?Yes, RainSign is part of the Raindrop Spend Management Suite requires a separate license. However, as part of the Raindrop AI powered platform it can be quickly deployed.
