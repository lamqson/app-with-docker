# Intake & Orchestration

### Turn scattered showers into seamless flow

#### Raindrop Intake & Orchestration

#### Raindrop Systems Intake & Orchestration creates one Digital Front Door for every procurement request—from quick supplier requests, to purchases to complex sourcing events. AI-powered routing, no-code workflows, and embedded compliance ensure requests follow the right path without manual triage. Unlike bolt-on tools, it connects to the full Source-to-Pay lifecycle: sourcing, contracts, supplier management, procurement, accounts payables and spend analytics.

### The Problem: Scattered Requests, Missed Opportunities

#### You’re probably dealing with scattered intake requests across email, Slack or MS Teams, and spreadsheets. A manager sends a message asking about a new supplier. A budget owner emails about software renewal. Someone in marketing uploads a contract to a shared drive and hopes Procurement finds it.

#### By the time your team gets visibility, commitments have already been made. The opportunity to negotiate, consolidate, or redirect? Gone.

#### The real cost of broken intake:

#### 30–40% of Procurement team time spent triaging and routing requests manually

#### 20–30% of organizational spend happening outside of Procurement visibility

#### Compliance gaps that lead to increased risk when required reviews are skipped or undocumented

#### Frustrated stakeholders who don’t know where their request stands

#### This isn’t a training problem or a discipline problem. It’s a systems problem. When it’s easier to go around procurement than through it, that’s exactly what happens.

### The Raindrop Systems Approach

#### Raindrop’s Intake & Orchestration creates a single Digital Front Door for every procurement request — from simple purchases to complex sourcing events.

#### Requests come in. The right workflows trigger automatically. Compliance is built in. And everything connects seamlessly to sourcing, contracts, suppliers, and spend.

#### Our approach is built on three principles:

#### 1. Make It Easy to Do the Right ThingIf intake is complicated, people bypass it. Raindrop makes submitting a request as simple as describing what you need. Rain, our AI Assistant, captures the right information and routes it automatically — no training required.

#### 2. Build Policy Into the ProcessCompliance isn’t an afterthought. Required reviews trigger automatically, approval paths adapt dynamically, and exceptions are flagged before they become issues.

#### 3. Connect Intake to ExecutionIntake only works if it drives outcomes. Raindrop connects every approved request directly into the full Source-to-Pay lifecycle — sourcing, contracts, supplier onboarding, purchasing, payables, and spend visibility — all in one unified platform.

## CORE CAPABILITIES

### Rain AI Assistant

#### Rain asks clarifying questions, captures requirements, and routes requests—without forms, without training, without friction. Rain gets smarter over time, learning from you.

### Multi-Channel Intake

#### Requests come from anywhere: configurable intake forms, Rain AI, Slack & Teams, email, or self-service catalogs with pre-approved items and suppliers available with minimal approval.

### Visual Orchestration Boards

#### Build and manage workflows without code: drag-and-drop configuration, real-time visibility, bottleneck identification, and a template library for common scenarios.

### Smart Routing

#### Requests route automatically based on value thresholds, category logic, risk triggers, parallel approvals, and escalation rules—your business rules, no code required.

### Policy Embedding

#### Compliance happens automatically: Security, Legal, and Finance reviews trigger based on request type. Documentation captured before approval. Every action logged and auditable. Exceptions flagged, not lost.

### Cross-System Orchestration

#### RainConnect integrates with SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics and other systems. Work alongside or replace current Procurement systems. SSO, automatic user provisioning, and two-way data sync.

## How It Works

### Request Capture

#### A stakeholder needs something—a new supplier, a software purchase, a contract renewal. They submit through [Rain AI](/section-5-examining-core-use-cases-for-agentic-ai-in-procurement-and-finance), an intake form, or an integrated channel. The system captures all relevant information upfront: what’s needed, who’s requesting, budget codes, timeline, and business justification.

### Intelligent Categorization

#### AI automatically determines request type (purchase, supplier onboarding, contract, sourcing event), category, value threshold, approval requirements, and risk level.

### Policy-Based Routing

#### Orchestration logic kicks in. The request routes to the right approvers and reviewers based on your business rules. Parallel workflows run simultaneously—legal, security, and finance can review at the same time.

### Cross-Functional Handoffs

#### Approved requests don’t stop at approval. Qualified requests trigger sourcing events. Approved suppliers route to onboarding workflows. Contracts route to execution and storage. Transactions sync to ERP for payment.

### Complete Audit Trail

#### Every action is tracked: who submitted, when, and what. What approvals were required and who provided them. What reviews were triggered. How long each step took. Final disposition and outcomes.

#### What is Raindrop Systems’ Intake and Orchestration?Raindrop Systems’ Intake and Orchestration is an embedded part of the Raindrop platform and provides a centralized system to manage and route all sourcing, purchasing, and contract intake requests. It ensures requests are consistently captured, automatically categorized, and directed to the right workflows and stakeholders—all as part of a unified Source-to-Pay platform.

#### How does Intake and Orchestration improve internal efficiency?By streamlining intake through structured forms or an AI Powered assistant Rain, Raindrop Systems uses AI-powered categorization and rule-based routing, to eliminate back-and-forth email, reduces miscommunication, and helps teams prioritize and manage requests with greater clarity and accountability. Teams typically save over 35% of time previously spent on manual triage.

#### Can the intake process be customized for different departments or request types?Yes. The system allows organizations to configure forms, approval paths, and routing logic to match different business needs—whether it’s IT Procurement, contract requests, marketing purchases, or sourcing events. Visual orchestration boards make configuration accessible without code.

#### Does intake and orchestration integrate with existing tools or workflows?Absolutely. RainConnect, our integration layer, connects seamlessly with ERPs (such as SAP, Oracle, NetSuite), collaboration tools (Slack, MS Teams), and existing Procurement systems. Data flows between systems automatically, eliminating manual handoffs and reducing integration work.

#### Who benefits most from using Intake and Orchestration?Procurement, Legal, IT, and Finance teams benefit by gaining visibility into intake demand, reducing turnaround times, and aligning intake with governance and compliance standards from the start. Business stakeholders benefit from simpler request submission and clear visibility into status.

#### How does Raindrop Systems’ approach differ from standalone intake tools?Most intake tools are orchestration layers that sit on top of disconnected systems. Raindrop Systems builds intake and orchestration as part of a unified Source-to-Pay platform. Approved requests connect directly to sourcing, contracts, supplier management, and spend analytics—no integration gaps, no manual handoffs.

#### What is Rain AI and how does it work?Rain is Raindrop Systems’ AI assistant embedded throughout the platform. For intake, Rain allows users to describe requests in natural language, asks clarifying questions, extracts information from documents, and routes requests automatically. Rain learns from your organization’s patterns and improves over time.

#### How long does it take to implement?Most customers go live with intake and orchestration as part of their standard module implementation, so in 2–4 weeks. Full Source-to-Pay implementations typically complete in 3–4 months. Our cloud-native architecture and pre-built integrations minimize IT lift and accelerate time-to-value.

#### What ROI can I expect?Organizations typically see 20–30% cost savings on request processing and achieve 50–75% automation rates within the first year. Teams using AI-native procurement platforms report 2.6× ROI and 58% faster cycle times . Most customers see measurable impact within their first quarter.

#### How do I measure success with Intake and Orchestration?Key metrics include request processing time, automation rate, first-time approval rate, compliance rate, and user adoption. Raindrop Systems’ analytics provide visibility into these metrics and help identify opportunities for continuous improvement.

#### Is Intake and Orchestration only for large enterprises?No. Mid-market organizations often see faster ROI because they have fewer resources to dedicate to manual triage. Raindrop Systems serves a range of medium to large enterprise customers, but finds a sweet spot with customers ranging from $250M to $2B in annual revenue, with pricing and implementation approaches scaled to organizational needs.
