# Supplier Management

### Storm-Proof Your Supplier Strategy

#### Raindrop Systems Supplier Management gives procurement teams a single source of truth for every supplier relationship—onboarding, compliance, performance, and collaboration in one place.

## KEY BENEFITS

### AI-Powered Onboarding

#### Rain, the AI assistant, guides requesters through conversational intake—collecting documents, routing approvals, and eliminating manual follow-up. Customers improve onboarding automation by up to 75%.

### Advanced Supplier Insights

#### Monitor financial health, compliance status, and industry risk factors in real time. Automated alerts flag expiring contracts, lapsing certifications, and regulatory issues before they become problems.

### Embedded Collaboration

#### Native chat, task assignments, and document sharing—inside the supplier record, not scattered across email and Slack. Every conversation is logged and searchable.

### The Problem: Supplier Data Everywhere, Visibility Nowhere

#### Your supplier data is scattered across ERPs, spreadsheets, shared drives, email threads, and somebody's inbox. Contract terms live in one system. Performance data sits in another. Insurance certificates? Good luck finding those after the person who managed them left.

#### You're not short on supplier data. You're short on supplier visibility.

#### What this actually costs you:

#### Slow onboarding — weeks of email back-and-forth for tax forms, compliance docs, and bank verification while the business unit waits

#### Compliance blind spots — contracts auto-renew unnoticed, certifications lapse, regulatory requirements get tracked in spreadsheets (when they get tracked at all)

#### No audit trail — supplier communication scattered across email, Slack, Teams, and phone calls with zero centralization

#### Manual everything — procurement and finance teams manually reviewing invoices, routing approvals, tracking performance in spreadsheets

#### According to [Ardent Partners](https://ardentpartners.com/), best-in-class organizations achieve 91% spend under management while the average sits at just 66%. That gap? It's where risk, waste, and missed opportunities live.

### The Raindrop Systems Approach

#### Raindrop Systems Supplier Management was built for teams who've had enough of stitching together supplier data from five different systems. It automates onboarding, ensures compliance, and gives you real-time visibility into every supplier relationship—all inside a [unified Source-to-Pay platform](/solutions/platform).

#### Three principles guide how we built it:

#### 1. Automate the Tedious, Focus on the StrategicSupplier onboarding shouldn't take weeks of email volleyball. Rain, our [AI assistant](/why-raindrop/ai-powered), guides requesters through conversational intake—collecting the right documents, asking the right questions, and routing approvals automatically. Your team focuses on supplier strategy instead of chasing paperwork.

#### 2. One Data Model, Not Five SystemsEvery supplier detail—profiles, contracts, invoices, payments, risk scores—lives in RainConnect, our unified data model. When procurement updates a supplier profile, legal and finance see it immediately. No duplicate data. No reconciliation. No "which spreadsheet is current?"

#### 3. Let Suppliers Help ThemselvesThe Supplier Marketplace gives vendors a single portal to manage their own profiles, submit documents, update certifications, and handle invoicing. Less administrative burden on your team. Fewer back-and-forth emails. Faster everything.

## Core Capabilities

## Supplier Marketplace

#### From registration to invoicing, suppliers manage everything in one connected portal. Self-service profile management, document submission, certification updates, and invoice tracking—all in one place. Your team stops chasing vendors for paperwork; vendors do it themselves.

## Real-Time Dashboards

#### Monitor supplier compliance, sustainability metrics, and operational risks through comprehensive dashboards. Track who’s buying from which supplier, how much is being spent, and when contracts expire—all in real time.

## Supplier Discovery

#### Find suppliers by commodity, category, or geography. Use AI-driven “looks like” search to identify vendors similar to your top performers. Risk signals surface during discovery—so you’re making informed decisions before the contract is signed, not after. Learn more about [supplier risk management within an S2P suite](/supplier-risk-management-within-an-s2p-suite).

### Additional Capabilities

#### AI-Powered Onboarding with RainYour [Digital Front Door](/solutions/platform/intake-orchestration) for every supplier request. Rain guides users through [conversational intake](/section-4-why-procurement-is-ideal-for-agentic-ai)—no 40-field forms, no training required. Dynamic workflows adapt to each supplier type: domestic vendors route differently from high-risk international suppliers. Customers improve onboarding automation by up to 75%.

#### 360-Degree Supplier ProfilesView every supplier relationship on one screen: sourcing events, active contracts, invoices, payments, insurance certificates, ESG data, compliance documentation. No switching between systems. No reconciling spreadsheets.

#### Advanced Supplier InsightsReal-time monitoring of supplier financial health, compliance status, and industry risk factors. You'll know when a supplier's financial health shifts, when certifications are about to expire, or when industry events could impact your supply chain—before it becomes a fire drill. Learn more about [supplier risk management within an S2P suite](/supplier-risk-management-within-an-s2p-suite).

[Microsoft Teams, Slack, and email integration](/solutions/platform/raindrop-integrates-anywhere) captures everything in the system of record.
