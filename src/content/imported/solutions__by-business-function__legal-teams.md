# Legal Teams

## Give Legal control over every contract — without becoming the bottleneck.

#### Raindrop pairs GenAI-assisted drafting, AI-powered first-pass review, and a searchable contract repository — so your team standardizes language, moves faster, and catches risk earlier, with Legal in control the whole way.

## The Problem

### Manual Contract Review Overload

#### Legal is expected to review every contract, but without automation, it’s slow and overwhelming.

### Lack of Visibility into Risk & Terms

#### Once a contract is signed, obligations often disappear into folders—creating risk and rework down the line.

### Too Little, Too Late

#### Legal often gets looped in after key terms are negotiated, reducing their ability to manage risk and enforce standards.

## The Solution

### Who It's For

#### In-house legal teams that need to standardize contract language, accelerate review, and keep risk under control as volume grows.

### What Legal governs

#### Your clause library, approved templates, fallback positions, and risk thresholds — the standards every contract is measured against.

### How Legal stays in control

#### Raindrop applies your standards automatically, routes only true exceptions to a lawyer, and keeps a full audit trail of who changed what and when.

### HOW RAINDROP HELPS

#### Raindrop empowers Legal teams with smart, scalable tools to standardize, accelerate, and de-risk the contract process. With GenAI-assisted contract creation and clause suggestions, you can generate first drafts instantly, aligned to your preferred language. Combine that with dynamic workflows, a central contract repository, and AI-powered review tools—and Legal becomes a strategic enabler, not a bottleneck.

### Outcomes that matter

#### Standards stay with Legal

#### Your clauses, playbooks, and risk thresholds govern every contract — automatically, across the whole lifecycle.

#### Routine review stops being a bottleneck

#### AI clears low-risk paper against your approved standards, so attorneys spend time only on the exceptions that need real judgment.

#### Risk is visible before it becomes exposure

#### Deviations, obligations, and renewal dates surface across your portfolio, with a full audit trail.

### What You Get With Raindrop

#### Gen AI-Powered Contract Drafting

#### Generate first drafts with your preferred clauses, tailored to deal type and risk profile—fast, consistent, and configurable.

#### Clause Libraries & Templates

#### Enforce your preferred language across the organization. Reuse what works, flag what doesn’t.

#### AI-Powered First-Pass Review

#### Automatically scan contracts for deviations from pre-approved terms to accelerate the review process.

#### Searchable Contract Repository

#### Quickly find obligations, renewal dates, and negotiated terms—no digging through PDFs scattered across systems.

#### Dynamic Approval Workflows

#### Route contracts for review based on risk level, value, or type—so you’re only pulled in when it counts.

#### Audit Trails & Access Controls

#### Track who touched what, when. Lock down sensitive info while enabling smooth collaboration.

### BONUS

#### Raindrop is fast to implement and intuitive to use. Legal teams can plug in with minimal disruption—and scale their influence —so Legal doesn’t have to wait months for value or endure a painful change management process.

### FAQ

#### How does Raindrop help legal teams reduce contract risk?Raindrop centralizes contract intake and lifecycle management, ensuring legal review happens at the right time, every time. With built-in approval workflows and a single system of record, it’s easier to enforce policy compliance and eliminate the risk of unauthorized or non-standard agreements slipping through.

#### How is our contract data kept secure?Raindrop is built with enterprise-grade security and role-based access controls and is [SOC 2 compliant.](/solutions/by-business-function/it-and-compliance-teams)

#### How does Raindrop improve visibility into contract status and compliance?Raindrop provides real-time tracking for every contract, with automated alerts and a full audit trail. Legal teams gain transparency into who’s touched a contract, where it stands, and whether it meets compliance standards—all without digging through email threads or shared drives.

#### Does Raindrop support collaboration across departments during the contract process?Yes. Raindrop bridges Legal, Procurement, Finance, and Business teams through a unified intake process and shared workflows — fostering collaboration while ensuring Legal maintains oversight and policy alignment.

#### What types of legal documents or processes can Raindrop help streamline?From NDAs and MSAs to complex vendor contracts and addendums, Raindrop supports a wide range of legal documents. Its configurable platform adapts to your specific legal processes, whether you're managing approvals, redlining drafts, or automating renewals.
