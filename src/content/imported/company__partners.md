# Partners

#### Raindrop partners with leading global and boutique consulting firms to deliver unmatched expertise and services across the Source-to-Pay journey.Together, we help customers move faster, unlock more value, and realize results with confidence—combining Raindrop’s modern platform with our partners’ deep domain knowledge and proven delivery capabilities.

# Resellers

# Our Reseller partners deliver a powerful combination of managed services and deep functional and industry expertise—powered by the Raindrop platform. They extend the value of Raindrop by embedding it into tailored solutions, helping customers accelerate adoption, streamline operations, and achieve measurable results.Advisory & Implementation

#### Our Advisory and Implementation Partners help co-sell and bring functional and industry expertise—along with change management and process improvement strategies—to help customers unlock the full value of Raindrop. Our Co-Sell partners deliver a powerful combination of managed services and deep functional and industry expertise—powered by the Raindrop platform.
