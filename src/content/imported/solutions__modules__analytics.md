# Analytics

### Clear the clouds

#### Raindrop Spend Intelligence gives you complete visibility across your spend ecosystem—turning disconnected data into strategic insight. Designed for procurement and finance leaders with out-of-the-box analytics and easy configurability, you can spot inefficiencies, make smarter decisions, and drive measurable impact.

## KEY BENEFITS

### See Everything, Clearly

#### Get a unified view of spend, suppliers, contracts, and payments—so you can identify hidden patterns, gaps, and opportunities.

### Make Smarter, Faster Decisions

#### Move beyond spreadsheets. With real-time dashboards and trusted data at your fingertips, you can make informed choices that save money and mitigate risk.

### Find Bottlenecks, Fuel Improvement

#### Identify inefficiencies across sourcing, contracting, and purchasing—then act on them to streamline processes and improve team performance.

## How It Works

## Unify Internal and External Data

#### Instantly connect data from ERPs and supplier systems with the rich sourcing, contract, invoice, and supplier data already captured in Raindrop—cleaned, normalized, and ready for analysis.

## Explore Insights, Out of the Box

#### Access built-in analytics for sourcing, supplier diversity, contract compliance, invoice cycle time, and more—no setup required.

## Configure What Matters

#### Tailor dashboards to your organization’s priorities—by category, geography, business unit, or strategic goals.

### From Visibility to Action

#### Raindrop helps you go beyond basic reporting with insights that fuel real outcomes:

#### Spot cost drivers and off-contract spend

#### Flag compliance and performance issues early

#### Track process delays and accelerate cycle times

#### Surface strategic savings and sourcing opportunities

#### Whether you need answers fast or want to dig deep, Raindrop makes insight accessible to everyone.

### FAQ

#### What does Raindrop Analytics help my organization measure?Raindrop Analytics delivers actionable insights into spend, supplier performance, contract utilization, and process bottlenecks. It enables procurement and finance leaders to track KPIs, monitor compliance, and uncover opportunities for savings or efficiency across the entire source-to-pay lifecycle.

#### Can I customize dashboards and reports in Raindrop Analytics?Yes. Raindrop offers highly configurable dashboards and self-service analytics tools. Users can tailor visuals, filters, and metrics to match their business goals without relying on IT or external consultants.

#### How does Raindrop Analytics support decision-making across teams?By providing real-time, role-based access to data, Raindrop empowers cross-functional stakeholders—procurement, finance, operations, and legal—to collaborate using a single source of truth. The insights help drive informed decisions and ensure alignment with strategic objectives.

#### What kind of data sources can Raindrop Analytics integrate with?Raindrop can integrate with ERPs, contract systems, procurement platforms, and financial tools to consolidate data from across your ecosystem. This unified view enhances visibility and enables more accurate trend analysis and forecasting.

#### How quickly can we start using Raindrop Analytics?Raindrop is designed for fast deployment with minimal lift. Many organizations begin seeing value in just weeks thanks to built-in templates, pre-configured dashboards, and guided onboarding tailored for procurement and finance professionals.
