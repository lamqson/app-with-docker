# IT and Compliance Teams

### WE KNOW YOUR PAIN

### Security and Compliance Risks

#### Every new platform is a potential vulnerability—and IT is responsible for making sure it’s airtight.

### Integration Headaches

#### Connecting new tools to your ERP, P2P, and other systems often requires time-consuming custom work.

### Heavy Implementation Lifts

#### New software rollouts usually mean big asks of IT—from setup to ongoing support.

### HOW RAINDROP HELPS

#### How Raindrop Helps: Raindrop is built with IT in mind—secure, scalable, and low lift. With robust access controls, audit trails, and enterprise-grade security, compliance is never an afterthought. Native integrations and flexible APIs make connecting to your ERP, P2P, and other systems simple. Best of all? Raindrop’s modular architecture and no-code configuration means IT won’t be buried in implementation work.

### WHAT YOU GET WITH RAINDROP

#### Enterprise-Grade Security: SOC 2 Type II, SSO/SAML, role-based access, encryption in transit and at rest—you name it.

#### Audit & Compliance Controls: Track every action with time-stamped logs. Easily support internal audits and policy enforcement.

#### Lightweight Implementation: Raindrop is designed to go live fast with minimal IT involvement—weeks, not months.

#### Native Integrations: Out-of-the-box connectors for major ERPs and procurement systems, plus open APIs for everything else. We’re fast, just ask us.

#### No-Code Configuration: Empower business users to make changes without submitting IT tickets or writing custom code.

### BONUS

#### Raindrop is built on a single code base on the Google Cloud Platform, with a unified UI and centralized controls—so as your organization expands usage, your IT burden doesn’t grow with it.

### FAQ

#### How does Raindrop help reduce the number of procurement tools our IT team needs to manage?Raindrop consolidates sourcing, contracting, intake, and supplier management into a single platform. This drastically reduces tool sprawl, simplifies integrations, and lessens the burden on IT teams responsible for maintaining fragmented systems.

#### Can Raindrop integrate with our existing enterprise tools and systems?Yes. Raindrop is designed with enterprise-grade integrations in mind, enabling seamless connectivity with ERPs, SSO platforms, and other key systems—reducing manual handoffs and preserving your current workflows.

#### How does Raindrop support compliance and audit-readiness?Raindrop tracks and documents every step in the sourcing and contract lifecycle, ensuring you have a centralized, searchable audit trail. Built-in controls and user permissions further support compliance with internal policies and regulatory requirements.

#### What measures does Raindrop take to ensure data security and user access control?Raindrop offers robust security features including role-based access, SSO integration, and detailed activity logs. These tools help IT and compliance teams maintain visibility, enforce policy, and safeguard sensitive procurement data.

#### How does Raindrop reduce shadow IT and rogue spending risks?By making it easy for users to engage with procurement processes through a unified digital front door, Raindrop encourages proper intake and approval channels—reducing the need for ad hoc or unsanctioned purchases that bypass IT and compliance oversight.
