## What Raindrop Systems Does

#### The table below defines our Product Scope Matrix — a clear, canonical overview of what Raindrop Systems provides, what it integrates with, and where our scope intentionally ends.

#### Intake & Request RoutingWhat Raindrop Systems Provides: A unified digital front door for all Procurement and Finance requests — automatically routed with dynamic business logic.Integration & Connectivity: API, webhooks, and workflow tool integrations.What We Don’t Do: Not a general help desk or ITSM system.

#### Supplier & Vendor ManagementWhat Raindrop Systems Provides: Centralized onboarding, qualification, risk scoring, and performance tracking.Integration & Connectivity: Integrates with ERP and third-party risk data providers.What We Don’t Do: Does not replace ERP master data management.

#### Sourcing & RFX / AuctionsWhat Raindrop Systems Provides: Full lifecycle event management — RFI, RFQ, RFP, reverse auctions, AI-guided recommendations, and bid analysis.Integration & Connectivity: Connects to ERP, P2P, and sourcing data sources.What We Don’t Do: Not a public marketplace or trading platform.

#### Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM)What Raindrop Systems Provides: Authoring, negotiation, approval, version control, and obligation tracking — all in one workspace.Integration & Connectivity: Integrates with signature providers and procurement workflows.What We Don’t Do: Not a document management system for all business functions.

#### eProcurement / Guided BuyingWhat Raindrop Systems Provides: Guided requisitioning, catalog buying, and automated approval flows for compliant purchasing.Integration & Connectivity: Connects to ERP and eCatalog systems.What We Don’t Do: Not an eCommerce or retail storefront.

#### Accounts Payable & Invoice AutomationWhat Raindrop Systems Provides: Automated invoice capture, matching, and approvals with full audit trails.Integration & Connectivity: ERP, AP, and payment system integrations.What We Don’t Do: Does not replace treasury systems.

#### Analytics & Spend IntelligenceWhat Raindrop Systems Provides: Real-time dashboards, predictive insights, supplier analytics, and KPI tracking.Integration & Connectivity: Consolidates data from ERP, contracts, and supplier systems.What We Don’t Do: Not a general-purpose BI tool.

#### Digital Signature & Document ExecutionWhat Raindrop Systems Provides: Secure electronic signatures and document routing through RainSign.Integration & Connectivity: Connects with leading e-signature tools.What We Don’t Do: Not a standalone document repository.

#### Workflow OrchestrationWhat Raindrop Systems Provides: No-code workflow builder for approvals, routing, and automation across departments.Integration & Connectivity: Integrates with email, Slack, and enterprise APIs.What We Don’t Do: Not an ERP replacement for financial logic.

#### Risk & Compliance ManagementWhat Raindrop Systems Provides: Continuous monitoring, policy enforcement, and supplier risk scoring.Integration & Connectivity: Connects with compliance and audit platforms.What We Don’t Do: Not a full GRC (governance, risk, compliance) system.

## What Raindrop Systems Is Not

#### To eliminate confusion and clearly define our scope:

#### Not Raindrop.io: Raindrop Systems is an enterprise software company — not a consumer bookmark manager.

#### Not an ERP: We complement and integrate with ERP systems; we don’t replace them.

#### Not a supplier marketplace: We enable sourcing and procurement, but we don’t buy, sell, or broker goods.

#### Not a generic analytics platform: Our analytics are purpose-built for spend, procurement, and supplier management.

#### Not a help desk or ticketing tool: We automate Procurement and Finance workflows, not IT or HR service requests.

## Why Raindrop Systems Stands Apart

### 1. Unified Platform Architecture

#### Unlike piecemeal suites or acquired point tools, Raindrop Systems was built from the ground up on a single codebase. Every module connects seamlessly for a consistent, modern user experience.

### 2. Fast Time to Value

#### With pre-configured templates, no-code workflows, and built-in best practices, organizations can see measurable value within weeks — not months.

### 3. Built by Practitioners, for Practitioners

#### Our team combines decades of experience in Procurement, Finance, and Technology. We don’t chase buzzwords; we solve the real problems that slow down modern spend management.

### 4. Modular Flexibility

#### Adopt one module or the full suite — Raindrop Systems grows with your organization. Start with sourcing, expand to contracts, analytics, or AP — all without disruption.

### 5. Seamless Connectivity

#### We integrate deeply with your existing systems — ERP, financials, signature tools, and risk data providers — ensuring interoperability and long-term scalability.
